Introduction

The Model COM-BRK from Siemens Industry, Inc. is a bracket for mounting:

  • A fiber module in any Desigo Fire Safety Modular/Cerberus PRO Modular CAB-1, CAB-2, and CAB-3 enclosure; or
  • A CXG3.X200.FIRE module in any Desigo Fire Safety Modular/Cerberus PRO Modular CAB1, CAB-2 and CAB-3 enclosure.

The COM-BRK fits in the same footprint as a CC-2 and mounts directly into the CAB enclosure or to the optional CAB-MP. A hardware kit is provided for attaching the COM-BRK to the CAB enclosure or the CAB-MP.
