Introduction

The FTS Series of FireFighter’s Telephone Stations from Siemens Industry, Inc., consists of the FTS, FTS-P, FTS-C, FTS-CL* and FTS-PCL* models. Any of the models in the FTS Series can mount into either the FB-300 flush backbox or the FB-301S surface backbox. The backboxes are mounted on the wall of the facility in accordance with local mounting codes in locations that provide easy access.

Wiring

  1. Begin with terminal 1 on the rear of the FTS telephone assembly and wire it to the negative position on the TZC-8B for the selected zone. (Refer to Figures 1 and 2 and the TZC-8B Installation Instructions, P/N 315-034110.)

  2. Newer versions of the FTS-CL and the FTS-PCL (identified by the presence of J1, see Figure 1) have new LED behavior. The LED now lights when the telephone circuit is connected at the command station, not when the handset is taken off hook.

  4. Continue with terminal 2 on the FTS and wire it to the positive position on the TZC-8B for the selected zone. (Refer to Figure 2 and the TZC-8B Installation Instructions, P/N 315-034110.)
  5. If no additional FTS telephones are installed, leave the end-of-line resistor on terminals 3 and 4. If there are additional telephones installed on the selected zone, place the resistor on the last phone in the zone. (Refer to Figure 2.)
  6. In Canada, remove all EOLs and use EOL P/N 140-0347726 (mount using Model EL-33).
  7. When wiring for the phone has been completed, secure the field wiring in place with the clamp on the rear of the telephone assembly. (See Figure 1.)

Electrical Ratings

Nominal Voltage: 30V

Max Current: 23mA

Mounting

Mount the FTS-Series telephone assembly in either the FB-300S flush backbox, P/N 500-680587, or the FB-301S surface backbox, P/N 500-624388, as shown in Figure 3 using the two #6-32 screws supplied.
