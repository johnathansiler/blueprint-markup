Introduction

The Models RL-HC (round plate) and RL-HW (rectangular plate) remote lamps from Siemens Industry, Inc., are compatible with models HFP-11, HFPT-11, OP921, OH921, HI921, OOH941, OOHC941, FDO421, FDOT421, FDT421, FDOOT441, and FDOOTC441 detectors. The red LED on the RL-HC or RL-HW lights when its associated detector goes into alarm.

Installation

The remote lamp may be placed up to 25 feet away from the detector using 14-18 gauge wire.

  • Use a single-gang switch box (user supplied) for mounting the RL-HW; Use a 4 inch octagonal conduit box (user supplied) for mounting the RL-HC. Make connections to the RLH as shown in Figure 1.
  • The RL-HC/RL-HW TB2 and TB3 are polarity insensitive.

If TB2 is connected to Detector (5), TB3 should be connected to Detector (6). Likewise, if TB2 is connected to Detector (6), TB3 should be connected to Detector (5).
