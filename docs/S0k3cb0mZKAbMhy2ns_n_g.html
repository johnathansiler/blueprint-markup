Introduction

XTRI-M Series Addressable Interface Module, P/N S54370-B4-A1, from Siemens Industry, Inc. shown in Figure 1, interfaces direct shorting devices to the device loop circuit of the Siemens Desigo and Cerberus PRO Fire Alarm Systems.

The XTRI-M module supports two operation modes: polarity insensitive mode and isolator mode. The module can be wired for either mode (refer to Figures 3 and 4). During the isolator mode, the built-in dual isolators will work at both sides of the module to isolate the line short in front or behind the module.

The XTRI-M can monitor a normally open or closed dry contact and it can report the status of the contacts.

WARNING

Observe precautions for handling Electrostatic Sensitive Devices.

Programming

Refer to Figure 2 to locate the black and white leads of the XTRI-M for device loop circuit wires.

Connect the Addressable Loop Driver circuit wires of the XTRI-M to the Model DPU Programmer/Tester. Use the cable provided with the Programmer/Tester and the two alligator clip to banana plug adapters provided.

CAUTION

To prevent damage to the DPU: DO NOT connect a XTRI-M to the DPU until all field wiring is removed from the device loop circuit wires of the XTRI-M.

Connection from the DPU to the XTRI-M is not polarity sensitive. Refer to Figures 6 and 7 for the proper connections to the control panel.

(Refer to Figure 2.) Follow the instructions in the DPU Programmer/Tester Manual (P/N 315-033260) to program the desired address into XTRI-M.

Add a logic function in the system configuration tool.

Record the device address on the label located on the XTRI-M. The XTRI-M can now be installed and wired to the system.

Wiring Switches and EOL

CAUTION

Deactivate P2 circuit by either or both of the following: Using the PMI, bypass the circuit being modified, and/or physically disconnect the circuit from the P2 source.

NOTES:

  1. There can be any number of normally closed or normally open switches.
  2. The end of line resistor must be located at the last switch.
  3. Do not wire a normally closed switch across the end of line resistor.
  4. Only for use with status applications.

NOTES:

  1. Mount EOL on the TB-EOL terminal.
  2. TB-EOL will not come with the package of product. Use Siemens TB-EOL, P/N S54322-F4-A2, or equivalent.
  3. For multi-normally closed switches monitoring, the open fault between switch will not be supervised.
  4. EOL 470 ohms 1% 1/2W will come with the package of product.

Wiring Module

(Refer to Figures 6 and 7) Refer to the wiring diagram and wire the XTRI-M addressable interface module accordingly.

Recommended wire size:
18 AWG minimum
14 AWG maximum

NOTES:

  1. All supervised switches must be held closed and/or open for at least a quarter of a second to guarantee detection.
  2. The drawing shown here is ONLY to supervise wire open, end of line device: 470 ohm, 1% 1/2W resistor, P/N A5Q00073045. Use Model TB-EOL with 470 ohm, 1% 1/2W resistor.
  3. When the XTRI-M is wired in polarity insensitive mode, Line 1 and Line 2 can be either line of the loop.
  4. The supervised switches have the following ratings:
    Voltage maximum: 27 VDC
    Current maximum: 6mA during polling
    Contact resistance maximum: 10 ohms
    Maximum cable length: 200 feet (18 AWG)
  5. CLine to line: 0.02uF
    Max line size: 14 AWG
  6. CLine to shield: 0.04uF
  7. Max line size: 14 AWG
  8. Min line size: 18 AWG
  9. CAUTION

    Ground shield of device line ONLY at the specified location on the Control Panel.

  10. CAUTION

    EOL device must be a 470 ohm, 1% 1/2W resistor for wire open supervision.

  11. The unshielded wire can be used at input to monitor the switch contact. No ground lead is provided.
  12. All circuits are power limited.
  13. Positive and negative ground fault detected at <25K ohms for the input wiring (ORANGE leads).
  14. In the device line, up to 30 of any compatible devices in polarity insensitive mode with 20 ohms max line resistance can be isolated between two modules in isolator mode in a Class A Style 6 wiring.
  15. In the device line, up to 30 of any compatible devices in polarity insensitive mode with 20 ohms max line resistance can be isolated behind one module in isolator mode in a Class B Style 4 wiring.
  16. HLIM isolator module and SBGA-34 sounder base cannot be used in the same loop with the modules in isolator mode.

Mounting

The Model XTRI-M mounts directly into a single gang switchbox (user supplied).

Connect the appropriate wires using wire nuts. Tuck the XTRI-M module inside the ULlisted/ recognized electrical box and dress the wiring as required. (See Figure 8.)
