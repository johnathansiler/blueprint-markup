Introduction

The Power Supply Filter Assembly from Siemens Industry, Inc., shown in Figure 1 filters the power from the incoming AC mains and distributes it to the PSC-12M power supply and the optional PSX-12M power supply extender. It also provides fuse protection to the mentioned power supplies. The Operating Temperature Range and Humidity is 0-49 C, and 93% @ 32 C. Install in accordance with National Electrical Code ANSI/NFPA 70, the Standard for Installation and Classification of Burglar and Holdup Alarm Systems, UL 681, and the Standard for Central Station Alarm Services, UL 827.

The terminal blocks of the PSFA are defined as follows:
TB1 AC: Main Input

WARNING

The main AC power line must be turned OFF prior to installation

The connectors of the PSFA are defined as follows:
P1: To PSC-12M, PSX-12M, PSC-12 or PSX-12
P2: To PSC-12M, PSX-12M, PSC-12 or PSX-12
P3: Laptop power connection (AC adapter, P/N 500-633992 required)

CAUTION

Always apply AC power first followed by the battery.

Installation

Refer to Figure 2.

Only one PSFA can be mounted in a CAB-1; one or two PSFAs can be mounted in a CAB-2BB/-RB or CAB-3BB/-RB.

  1. Place the four ¼” holes on the PSFA over the threaded studs in the desired location in the bottom portion of the CAB enclosure.
  2. Secure the PSFA in place with the four #10 hex nuts supplied.

Wiring

WARNING

Disconnect BATTERY and AC prior to working on equipment.

Refer to Figure 3.

Wire in accordance with local codes and Article 760 of the NEC, NFPA latest edition. In compliance with NEC, all power limited fire protective signaling conductors must be separated a minimum of a 1/4" from all of the following wiring located within a control panel:

  • Electric light
  • Power
  • Class 1 or non-power limited fire protective signaling conductor

Refer to the CAB1 Installation Instructions, P/N 315-033007, or the CAB2-BB/-RB and CAB3-BB/-RB Installation Instructions, P/N 315-033009, as applicable for wiring requirements to comply with NEC codes.

TB1 comes equipped with a press-fit 3-position protective cover that must be removed to make connections in TB1. Make sure it is put back properly when finished.

2. Connect the Green (earth ground) wire from the AC mains to the earth ground lug on the enclosure (chassis) and then connect a locally provided wire (14 AWG minimum, 10 AWG maximum) from the ground lug to TB1-G (Earth Ground) terminal.

You must install the PSC-12M/PSX-12M/PSC-12/PSX-12 in the row and position directly above the PSFA that connects to it.

3. The 12-inch AC wire assembly connects the PSFA to the PSC-12M/PSX12M/PSC-12/PSX-12. Plug one end of this AC assembly cable into P12 on the PSC-12M/PSX-12M/PSC-12/PSX-12. Plug the other end of the cable into P1 or P2 on the PSFA.

4. When two PSFAs are used in the same enclosure (CAB2-BB/-RB or CAB3-BB/-RB only), wire as shown in Figure 3 using 14 AWG minimum, 10 AWG maximum gauge wire.

5. Tighten the screw terminals of TB1 and gently pull AC connection to verify that the AC line is properly connected to the PSFA.

Only one PSFA can be mounted in a CAB-1; one or two PSFAs can be mounted in a CAB-2BB/-RB or CAB-3BB/-RB.

For battery recommended replacements, refer to PSC-12M installation Instructions, Document ID A6V12021370.For recommended battery replacement maintenance, please refer to the System Label, P/N A5Q00075144

Post-Installation

Verify fuses and protective cover:

The PSFA comes pre-installed with two 5A fuses (use only UL/ULC recognized 5A, 3AG or 3AB, little fuse 312005, 314005 or 332005) in Fuse holders F1 and F2. Make sure that the fuses are properly secured in its respective fuse holder as they might have dislodged during shipping.

Make sure that the press-fit 3-position protective cover in TB1 is put back properly for safety reasons.

WARNING

The green earth ground wire from the AC Mains MUST be connected to the “EARTH” ground lug located in the enclosure directly below where the PSFA is mounted.
