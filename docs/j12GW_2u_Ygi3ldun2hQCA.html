Introduction

The REMBOX2/R and REMBOX4/R Enclosures are used to house a Remote Person Machine Interface (PMI/PMI-2/PMI-3), an SSD-C-REM or CAN modules (LVM/FCM/ LCM/SCM/OCM/SIM). A Fireman’s Master Telephone (FMT) can also be installed in the REMBOX4/R. The REMBOX2 and REMBOX4 backbox and doors are black; the REMBOX2R and REMBOX4R backbox and doors are red. They are identical in all other ways, and will be referred to in the remainder of this document as REMBOX2 and REMBOX4. The REMBOX consists of an outer door, an inner door, and a backbox as shown in Figure 1.

REMBOX2 and REMBOX4 Enclosures are for indoor use only in dry environments.

The outer door and inner door are permanently hinged left. The REMBOX has a 3/4 inch flange on all four sides of the enclosure which is used for flush mounting applications.

The backbox is mounted on a flat surface with four user-supplied bolts that are a maximum of ¼ inch in diameter.

Installation

Prior to installation consider the following:

  • Mounting height for visual and manual access to the Remote Person Machine Interface (PMI/PMI-2/PMI-3) displayed in the opening of the outer door.
  • Weight and size of the enclosure.
  • Local codes.

Install the backbox:

  1. Select a clean, dry, shock and vibration free surface.
  2. Position the backbox clear of obstructions so that the front door opens freely and the controls and indicators are easily accessible.

  3. When preparing the opening to flush mount the REMBOX, make sure it does not exceed the dimensions of the enclosure. (Refer to the SPECIFICATIONS section for dimensions.)

  4. Mark the locations of the two upper mounting bolts of the backbox on the wall. (Refer to Figure 2.)
  5. Drill the two holes located in the previous step and screw in the top bolts, leaving a small gap between the wall and each top bolt.
  6. Place the backbox over the two top bolts and allow it to slide down over the bolts.
  7. Mark, drill, and install the two bottom bolts in the backbox.
  8. Tighten all four bolts securely against the back wall of the backbox.

Wiring

In compliance with NEC Article 760, all power limited fire protective signaling conductors must be separated a minimum of ¼ inch from all of the following wiring located within a control panel:

  • electric light
  • power
  • Class 1 or non-power limited fire protective signaling conductors

To meet these requirements, the following guidelines must be observed when installing modules and wiring to this control panel.

When installing power limited field wiring, the installer must comply with NEC article 760-54, which states:

The fire alarm power-limited circuits are installed using Types FPL, FPLR, FPLP or permitted substitute cable, provided these power-limited cable conductors extending beyond the jacket are separated by a minimum of 0.25 in. (6.35 mm) or by a nonconductive sleeve or nonconductive barrier from all other conductors.

Wiring Entering Enclosure

Power Limited Wiring

All wiring entering the REMBOX is considered power limited. Wiring must be in the shortest route and must not overlap any other wiring.

Install Wiring

Wire in accordance with local codes and NEC 760.

  1. Remove the knockouts in the backbox for the entry of field wiring. (There are two knockouts each in the top and bottom of the REMBOX2 and REMBOX4.)
  2. Pull all field wiring into the backbox. Do not dress the wiring until the location of all the equipment is known.

Post-Installation

After the backbox and wiring are installed, complete the installation of the REMBOX by installing the RNI, the optional tamper switch, the PMI/PMI-2/PMI-3 or SSD-CREM, any optional modules (FCM-6, LCM-8, SCM-8, OCM-16, SIM-16, FMT, LVM), and the outer door.

  1. Install the RNI on the mounting plate in the backbox. Refer to the RNI Installation Instructions, P/N 315-033420. Refer to Figure 3 for the location of the RNI mounting plate.
  2. (Optional) Install the HTSW-1 Tamper Switch. Refer to the HTSW-1 Installation Instructions, P/N 315-033350. Refer to Figure 3 for the location of the tamper switch knockout slots.
  3. Install the PMI/PMI-2/PMI-3 or SSD-C-REM. Refer to the PMI/PMI-INTL Installation Instructions, P/N 315-033070, the PMI-2 Installation Instructions, P/ N 315-050636, the PMI-3 Installation Instructions, Document ID A6V10446194, or the SSD-C-REM Installation Instructions, P/N 315-034773, as applicable.
  4. Install any optional OCM-16 or SIM-16 modules using the REMBOX2/4-MP mounting plate. Refer to the REMBOX2/4-MP Installation Instructions, P/N 315-034211
  5. Install any optional modules (FCM-6, LCM-8, SCM-8, FMT, LVM) in the inner door of the REMBOX. Refer to the FCM-6 / LCM-8 / SCM-8 Installation Instructions (P/N 315-033040), FMT Installation Instructions (P/N 315-064100) or LVM Installation Instructions (P/N 315-034090).
  7. Insert blank plates (ID-SP), as needed in the REMBOX2 or REMBOX4, in unused spaces on the inner door and secure with 10-32 nuts. (Refer to Table 1.)
  8. Close the inner door. The inner door can be secured with a 6-32 x 1/2” screw, if desired.
  9. Install the outer door by placing it on its hinges. Close the outer door and secure with key.
